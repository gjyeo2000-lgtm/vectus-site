CORE MODELS

Different calculations for different questions

Statistical models, product-relationship analysis, a trained neural network, and explicit rules have different roles. No single model applies to every screen.

01

Regime and trend

Statistical models such as HMM examine regimes and signs of change for eligible subjects with sufficient history.

02

Asset relationships

Calculate common exposure across supported products and registered holdings. Not all relationship analysis is a trained GNN.

03

ETF risk propagation

A trained GNN uses overlapping ETF constituents to adjust ETF allocation caps during EMP construction. This is a limited path.

04

Judgment safeguards

Explicit rules distinguish insufficient observations, date mismatches, and risk conditions before a judgment or deferral.

INSURANCE DIAGNOSIS

We put the coverage you may already have before another sale

Customer coverage and rights, not enrollment commissions, are our starting point. We match your policies to official terms for the right product edition and mark unsupported conclusions for review.

Our goal is to use official terms to identify important riders and claim conditions you might miss, explain them clearly, and reduce repetitive diagnosis work.

CUSTOMER FIRST

Understand the riders and claim conditions you might miss

We are building a service that systematically explains important riders, claim conditions, and limitations that are easy to miss in complex policy documents. By reducing repetitive policy searches and edition matching, we aim to help customers identify coverage they may be entitled to and prepare a well-founded claim. Actual payment depends on policy terms and claim assessment.
