CORE MODELS

A dedicated model for every problem

02

Connectivity risk analysis

Calculates overlap risk and complementary effects across your holdings.

03

Multi-factor optimization

Adjusts return relative to risk.

04

Trend reversal detection

Statistically identifies when market direction is about to change.

05

News impact analysis

Tracks how news affects your holdings using rules — not an LLM's free interpretation.

06

Five-axis combined judgment

"A good company" and "a good price right now" are different things. We weigh financial strength, fair value, trend, flow, and short pressure together, and tell you why to hold off when they disagree.

BEYOND THE RECOMMENDATION

The recommendation isn't the end

Most places stop at the recommendation. Vectus keeps going.

01

Intraday 5-slot price forecast

Delivers a statistically calibrated near-term price range at each key intraday checkpoint for individual stocks.

02

Volatility-analog analysis

Finds past trading days with a similar volatility pattern and shows what actually happened afterward.

03

Recommendation history

We don't stop at the recommendation. We track whether past recommendations were actually right, using 5/20/60-day returns.

04

Custom stock screener

Combine 6 core judgment factors yourself — volatility, profitability, trend reversal, and more — to filter for exactly the stocks you want.

05

Custom alert conditions

Set your own alerts — target price reached, a loss threshold hit, or a judgment grade change.

06

Dividend-focused stocks

We don't just show dividend yield. See the estimated pre-tax payout on a ₩10M position, this dividend's record date, ex-dividend recovery time, and dividend streak alongside payout ratio.

07

Quarterly earnings reversals

Full-year results alone won't show this. We work backward across every stock to find a profitable year that hid a loss-making fourth quarter, and show the price reaction after disclosure.
