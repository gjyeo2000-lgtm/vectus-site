Beyond a list of products

Count shared assets, not just product labels.

Two products may expose you to the same underlying asset. Vectus compares composition dates with registered holdings and separates verified overlap from unverified portions.

  • Connect products only where composition and dates are available.
  • The analysis covers customer-registered holdings and supported composition.
  • Missing composition or identifiers do not prove the absence of overlap.

Different products, different sources

Do not merge ETF prices and public-fund reference prices into one timestamp.

An ETF market price and its net asset value can differ in value and timing. A public-fund reference price also needs its disclosure and effective date checked. Composition, prices, and dates therefore remain separate.
