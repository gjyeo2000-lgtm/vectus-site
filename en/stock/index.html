How it works

Make the reason for a decision inspectable.

The useful answer is more than a one-line result. Check which source and date were used, whether the observations were sufficient, and why a judgment was deferred.

  • First check the supported stock, source, and date.
  • Do not treat prices and filings from different effective dates as one fact.
  • If observations or linkage are insufficient, do not force a conclusion.

What the customer sees

A supported judgment and a deferral are different outcomes.

For example, a newly verified filing may take effect after the price date. It cannot be presented as already reflected in that earlier price. Vectus separates the dates and evidence, leaving what needs another check visible.
